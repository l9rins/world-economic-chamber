Charter, Policies and Governance

CHARTER AND GOVERNANCE DOCUMENTS

[Note to Mark: Only the texts in bold below should be clickable.]

The World Economic Chamber’s Charter and governance documents form the institutional framework through which the organisation carries out its responsibilities. They provide members with a clear understanding of how the Chamber operates, how decisions are made and the standards expected of all organisations that participate in its work. This suite of documents reflects a disciplined approach to international engagement—one that prioritises integrity, independence and respect for the regulatory environments in which members conduct their cross‑border activities.

These documents establish the Chamber’s mandate, define its governance architecture and set out the ethical and operational expectations that guide all WEC initiatives. They ensure that the organisation functions with consistency and transparency, offering members a stable institutional counterpart in an increasingly complex global economic landscape. By maintaining a coherent and professionally structured governance framework, the Chamber reinforces its commitment to responsible international business conduct and long‑term economic cooperation.
