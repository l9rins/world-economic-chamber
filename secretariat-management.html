Secretariat Management

EXECUTIVE SECRETARIAT OPERATIONAL MANAGEMENT PROTOCOLS

1. Purpose and Scope

The Executive Secretariat Operational Management Protocols define how the Secretariat manages the World Economic Chamber’s daytoday operations, administrative functions and crossjurisdictional coordination.

These protocols establish the standards, processes and conduct expectations that ensure Secretariat activities are disciplined, neutral and aligned with the Chamber’s governance framework. They apply to all Secretariat units and personnel, regardless of jurisdiction, and serve as the operational foundation for consistent institutional execution.

2. Operational Principles

Secretariat operations are guided by three core principles:

Professional Discipline – All activities must be executed with precision, consistency and adherence to approved procedures.

Neutrality – Secretariat conduct must remain impartial and free from external influence across all jurisdictions.

Transparency – Operational actions, documentation and communication must be clear, accurate and retrievable.

These principles ensure that Secretariat operations reinforce institutional credibility and support effective governance oversight.

3. Secretariat Mandate

The Secretariat is responsible for the Chamber’s administrative and operational execution. Its mandate includes:

Managing daily institutional operations

Coordinating crossborder and interjurisdictional activities

Maintaining documentation, records and reporting obligations

Supporting leadership with timely information and analysis

Ensuring compliance with governance protocols and decisionmaking pathways

The Secretariat acts as the operational centre of the Chamber, ensuring continuity, coherence and disciplined execution.

4. Operational Management Responsibilities

4.1 Administrative Management

The Secretariat must maintain structured administrative processes that support institutional stability. Responsibilities include:

Managing internal workflows, schedules and coordination mechanisms

Maintaining governance records, operational files and institutional archives

Ensuring administrative actions comply with documentation standards

Supporting leadership with administrative preparation for meetings, consultations and engagements

4.2 Program and Activity Execution

The Secretariat is responsible for executing approved programs and activities. This includes:

Implementing operational tasks arising from Council or leadership decisions

Coordinating logistics for crossborder engagements, consultations and institutional events

Ensuring program execution follows approved protocols and timelines

Documenting progress, outcomes and operational issues

4.3 Communication and Information Management

The Secretariat must maintain disciplined communication practices. Responsibilities include:

Ensuring internal communication is accurate, timely and aligned with governance protocols

Managing information flows between leadership, oversight bodies and operational units

Preparing institutional correspondence, reports and briefing materials

Safeguarding sensitive information and ensuring compliance with confidentiality requirements

4.4 Compliance and Protocol Adherence

The Secretariat must ensure that all operational actions comply with institutional governance standards. This includes:

Following approved decisionmaking pathways

Adhering to neutrality and independence safeguards

Ensuring documentation meets audit and retrieval requirements

Reporting compliance issues to leadership or oversight functions

5. Coordination Responsibilities

5.1 Coordination with Leadership

The Secretariat must maintain structured coordination with Executive Leadership. Responsibilities include:

Providing leadership with timely operational updates and reports

Seeking guidance when operational issues require escalation

Ensuring leadership directives are implemented consistently across jurisdictions

Supporting leadership oversight activities with accurate documentation

5.2 Coordination Across Jurisdictions

The Secretariat ensures operational coherence across all jurisdictions in which the Chamber operates. Responsibilities include:

Maintaining consistent operational standards across borders

Coordinating crossjurisdictional activities and information flows

Ensuring local operational actions comply with global governance protocols

Reporting jurisdictionspecific issues that may affect institutional operations

5.3 Coordination with Oversight Functions

The Secretariat must support oversight and compliance activities. Responsibilities include:

Providing documentation and information required for oversight reviews

Implementing corrective actions arising from oversight findings

Reporting operational risks or compliance issues promptly

Maintaining transparency in all oversightrelated communication

6. Documentation and Reporting Standards

The Secretariat must maintain disciplined documentation practices consistent with the RecordKeeping & Documentation Standards. Requirements include:

Documenting all operational actions, decisions and communications

Maintaining accurate records of crossborder engagements and consultations

Ensuring reports are complete, timely and aligned with governance expectations

Preserving a clear audit trail for all operational activities

Documentation is essential for transparency, accountability and institutional continuity.

7. Operational Risk Management

The Secretariat must identify and manage operational risks that may affect institutional stability or crossborder credibility. Responsibilities include:

Monitoring operational environments for emerging risks

Reporting risks to leadership and oversight functions

Implementing mitigation measures as directed

Documenting risk assessments and followup actions

Risk management ensures that Secretariat operations remain stable, predictable and aligned with institutional principles.

8. Conduct Expectations

Secretariat personnel must demonstrate conduct consistent with the Chamber’s institutional standards. Expectations include:

Acting impartially and professionally in all engagements

Avoiding conflicts of interest and influence pressures

Maintaining confidentiality and safeguarding sensitive information

Upholding the Chamber’s independence and neutrality at all times

These expectations ensure that Secretariat conduct reinforces institutional credibility.

9. Alignment with Institutional Governance

These protocols ensure that Secretariat operations:

Support disciplined governance and structured oversight

Maintain consistency across jurisdictions

Uphold neutrality, independence and professionalism

Reinforce the Chamber’s longterm stability and institutional integrity

Leading global economic growth through robust governance and strategic oversight.
